Veil Reads ("the Service") is operated by Vidalsocial Tech ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information is collected when you visit veilreads.com and how it is used. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Veil Reads does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or other identifying details in order to use the Service.

  • Information you provide. If you choose to enter a focus or intention before a reading, that text is submitted along with your request so a reading can be generated for you. Entering a focus is optional.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we collect standard usage data through analytics and similar technologies — such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and the site that referred you.

How Your Readings Are Generated

When you request a reading, the cards drawn and any focus text you enter are transmitted to our server and to a third-party artificial-intelligence provider (Anthropic) for the sole purpose of generating the written reading you receive. This information is used to produce your reading and is not used to identify you or to build an advertising profile.

Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the Service:

  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Service. It sets cookies that collect usage information in aggregate.
  • Advertising. We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to the Service and elsewhere on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info. You can also manage or block cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended if you do.

Third-Party Services

The Service relies on third parties, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics & Google AdSense — measurement and advertising
  • Anthropic — artificial-intelligence processing used to generate readings
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery

Your Choices

You can control cookies through your browser, opt out of personalized ads using the links above, and choose not to enter a focus before a reading. Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal; because there is no industry standard for responding to it, the Service does not currently respond to such signals.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so it can be removed.
